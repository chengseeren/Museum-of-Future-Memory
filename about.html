About the Archive

Museum of Future Memories

This archive preserves futures before they exist. Inspired by the participatory spirit of the Whole Earth Catalog and speculative publishing systems, the project invites visitors to leave behind visions, memories, and traces of the worlds they hope to create together.

In this archive, we not only preserve the past, but also collect people’s shared visions of the future.

We hope visitors will leave behind memories,wishes, photographs, messages, and small tracesof the worlds they imagine for their future lives.Some may be quiet and personal moments, while others may describe hopeful cities filled with greenery, slower ways of living,cleaner environments, or new ways of caring for both the planet and the people around us.

Rather than presenting a single vision of the future, this archive is shaped collectively by everyone who contributes to it. Each uploaded memory becomes part of a growing network of ideas, emotions, and possibilities — a reminder that the future is not something fixed, but something we are constantly creating together.

By sharing these imagined memories of the future, the project hopes to gently remind visitors of the importance of environmental awareness and collective responsibility. Not through fear or loss, but through the belief that a better future is still possible, and that it is something worth protecting, imagining, and remembering together.
